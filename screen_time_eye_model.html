    How this tool works — and where it stops

    This is a research-anchored estimator, not a diagnostic or a personal prediction. It combines results from different studies with different populations, definitions of "digital eye strain," and measurement methods. Cross-study comparisons here are illustrative, not a unified regression fit to individual-level data — no such public dataset exists. Associations reported in the source studies (especially for myopia) are correlational; screen time is one contributing factor among many, including genetics, near-work in general, and time spent outdoors. Nothing here should replace an eye exam or a clinician's advice, and if you're experiencing persistent eye pain, sudden vision changes, or headaches, see an eye care professional rather than relying on this tool.
